About Feynman Lab

A lab for ideas worth making real.

People have ideas. We make some of them real.

Curiosity, put to work.

Feynman Lab is a small experimental software lab. People send us ideas for tools, websites, automations, and other useful pieces of software they wish existed.

We choose a few useful, feasible ideas that fit our time and curiosity. A submission does not guarantee that we will build it.

When we release something, we want it to be usable by more people. We will share the problem, the working tool, and source code when possible.

Who is behind the lab?

Feynman Lab is an independent software project. You can inspect the website source and follow public conversations through the Feynman Lab GitHub organization, or contact us through the email on the Submit page. We are at the beginning and have not published a founder profile yet.

See the public GitHub organization ↗

Why no development fee?

We choose ideas ourselves and build tools intended for wider use. That freedom to choose is what makes the no-fee model possible. The person who suggests an idea is a contributor to the possibility, not a client commissioning private software.

This is an early-stage project, not an established revenue stream. Over time, we hope useful tools and their stories will bring readers and users to the site. Advertising and possibly other revenue that does not charge submitters could support more development. We will be clear about it if that changes.
