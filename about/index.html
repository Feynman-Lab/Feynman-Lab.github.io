About Feynman Lab

A lab for ideas worth making real.

People have ideas. We make some of them real.

Curiosity, put to work.

Feynman Lab is a small experimental software lab. People send us ideas for tools, websites, automations, and other useful pieces of software they wish existed.

When we find an idea that's useful, feasible, and interesting, we may build it for free. We choose projects around the time and resources we have, so submitting an idea doesn't guarantee we'll make it.

We believe good small tools can make a real difference. As projects take shape, we'll share what we build and, whenever possible, make the code available for others to learn from and improve.
