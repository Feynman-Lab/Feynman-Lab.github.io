What fits the lab?

Good fits are focused web tools, browser extensions, automations, and file or data utilities that could help more than one person.

Private business systems, large apps, urgent deadlines, and highly personal custom work are unlikely to fit.

What happens next?

We review ideas for usefulness beyond one person, feasibility, scope, and available time. If we want to explore yours, we will contact you to discuss it. We cannot promise a reply or a timeline for every submission.

Submitting an idea is not acceptance. Selected projects are built without a development fee, but they are lab projects, not commissioned work.

Privacy and publication

Email submissions go to our inbox and are not public. Your email address lets us reply; please do not send passwords or confidential material.

GitHub Discussions are public and require a GitHub account. Anything you post there can be read by anyone.

A selected idea may become a public tool, and its code may be open source. We will discuss the scope before building and ask before publishing your private message or identifying you.
