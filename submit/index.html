Start with an idea

Tell us what you need.

It can be a small tool, a website, an extension, an automation, or something harder to name. Explain the problem in your own words.

Share your idea

No technical plan needed.

A few details help us understand it:

  1. What would you like to exist?
  2. What problem would it solve, and for whom?
  3. Do you have an example or reference?
Send your idea by email ↗

This opens your email app with a few prompts. You can also write directly to torres4koo@gmail.com.

Prefer to share it publicly?Post on GitHub Discussions ↗

GitHub sign-in is required. Public posts can be read by anyone, so please don't include sensitive information.

What happens next?

We review requests and choose projects that are useful, realistic to build, and a good fit for our available time. If we choose yours, we'll get in touch to understand the details.

Submitting an idea is free, and selected projects are built for free. We can't promise to build every request or give each one a deadline.
