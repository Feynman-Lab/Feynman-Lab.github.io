An experimental software lab

Ideas into reality.

Have a small software idea? Suggest it to the lab. We select a few useful, feasible ideas and turn them into tools others can use. There is no development fee for selected projects.

Anyone can suggest an idea. We decide what to explore and build.
Why we exist

Good ideas deserve a chance to exist.

Sometimes a useful tool is too small for a company to make and too specific for an existing product to solve. That's exactly the kind of possibility we like exploring.

Why is it free?

We choose what to build.

Feynman Lab is an independent project. Suggesting an idea costs nothing, and we do not take commissions. We choose ideas that could help more people, then share what we make. The lab is still at the beginning; our longer-term support model is explained on the About page.

The process

Small ideas can go a long way.

You don't need a polished pitch or a technical plan. Start with a problem you want to solve.

01 / TELL US

Describe your idea

Tell us what you wish existed and why it would help.

02 / WE CHOOSE

We look for a fit

We choose ideas based on usefulness, feasibility, and the time we have.

03 / WE BUILD

Make it real

If we select an idea, we shape and build a useful public tool. We do not take on custom development requests.

A good fit for the lab

Small software. Real use.

We look for specific problems that could become useful, standalone tools. These are the kinds of ideas that fit our lab.

Web tools

A focused website that helps someone complete one useful task.

Browser extensions

A small improvement to the way people work on the web.

Automation scripts

A repeatable task that software could take off someone's hands.

Data utilities

A practical converter or helper for files, text, images, or data.

We choose projects based on usefulness, feasibility, and available time. Submitting an idea does not guarantee we'll build it.
